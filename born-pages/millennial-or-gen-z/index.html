Quick answer

Using the widely cited Pew Research Center boundary, people born from 1981 through 1996 are Millennials, while people born from 1997 onward belong to the following generation, commonly called Gen Z. The Library of Congress research guide presents the commonly used Gen Z range as 1997–2012. Generation boundaries are analytical conventions, not universal legal or scientific classifications.

What does “Zillennial” mean?

“Zillennial” is an informal overlap label sometimes used by people born near the Millennial–Gen Z boundary. It is not a standardized research category, and different writers use different year ranges. Someone born in 1995, 1996, 1997 or 1998 may relate to experiences associated with both groups, even when a chart assigns one generation label.

Your birth-year classification is a convenient research label. It does not determine your personality, values, habits or identity.

Why do some websites use different dates?

  • There is no single government authority that defines every generation after the Baby Boom.
  • Researchers may choose boundaries that fit the historical event or population they are studying.
  • Popular culture often adopts names and ranges before researchers settle on a working definition.
  • A one- or two-year difference does not make one source automatically wrong; the source should explain its method.

This page uses the Pew 1981–1996 Millennial endpoint and the Library of Congress guide's 1997–2012 Gen Z range for a clear, consistent answer.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1996 Millennial or Gen Z?

Under Pew Research Center's working definition, 1996 is the final Millennial birth year.

Is 1997 Millennial or Gen Z?

Under the same definition, 1997 is the first year of the generation following Millennials, commonly called Gen Z.

Is 1995 a Millennial?

Yes. A person born in 1995 falls within the commonly used 1981–1996 Millennial range.

Is 1998 Gen Z?

Yes. A person born in 1998 falls within the commonly used Gen Z range beginning in 1997.

Can I identify as a Zillennial?

Zillennial is an informal cultural label rather than a standardized research category. People near the boundary may use it if it reflects their experiences.
