Are 1980s babies Gen X or Millennials?

Both groups appear in the decade. The year 1980 is commonly treated as the final Generation X birth year, while 1981 begins the commonly used Millennial range. Generation labels are broad research categories, not rules that determine identity.

The oldest people in this decade are 45 before their birthday and 46 on or after it. The youngest are 36 before their birthday and 37 on or after it.

Frequently asked questions

How old are 1980s babies in 2026?

They are between 36 and 46 during 2026. The exact answer depends on the birth year and whether the birthday has already occurred.

How old is someone born in 1985 in 2026?

They are 40 before their birthday and 41 on or after their birthday in 2026.

What generation are people born in the 1980s?

Someone born in 1980 is Generation X under the 1965–1980 range. People born from 1981 through 1989 are Millennials under the 1981–1996 range.
