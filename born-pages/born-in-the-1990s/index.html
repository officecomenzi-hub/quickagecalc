Quick answer

If you were born from 1990 through 1999, you are 26 to 36 years old during 2026, depending on your birth year and birthday. Under the working ranges used by QuickAgeCalc, 1990–1996 are Millennials and 1997–1999 are Generation Z.

Are 1990s babies Millennials or Gen Z?

Both. The commonly used Millennial range ends in 1996 and the Gen Z range begins in 1997. That makes 1996 and 1997 the key boundary years in this decade. The focused Millennial or Gen Z checker explains the cutoff and nearby years.

The oldest people in this decade are 35 before their birthday and 36 on or after it. The youngest are 26 before their birthday and 27 on or after it.

How old are 1990s babies in 2026?
