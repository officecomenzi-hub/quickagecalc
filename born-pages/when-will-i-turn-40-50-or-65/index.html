When Will I Turn 40, 50 or 65?

Enter your date of birth to find the exact calendar dates for three major age milestones and see how much time remains.

Last reviewed: July 28, 2026

Quick answer

Add 40, 50 or 65 to your birth year. Your milestone normally falls on the same month and day as your birthday. For example, someone born on June 15, 1990 turns 40 on June 15, 2030, turns 50 on June 15, 2040 and turns 65 on June 15, 2055.

Why the full birth date matters

The birth year tells you the milestone year, but the month and day determine the exact milestone date and whether that birthday has already occurred.

Planning around milestone ages

  • Age 40: often used as a personal or career milestone.
  • Age 50: may be relevant to long-term financial, health or family planning.
  • Age 65: is commonly associated with retirement planning, but official pension and benefit rules vary by country and programme.

This page calculates calendar dates only. It does not determine legal eligibility, pension rights, retirement dates or benefit entitlements.

Frequently asked questions

When will I turn 40?

Add 40 years to your date of birth. Someone born on October 3, 1992 turns 40 on October 3, 2032.

When will I turn 50?

Add 50 years to your date of birth. Someone born on May 20, 1985 turns 50 on May 20, 2035.

When will I turn 65?

Add 65 years to your date of birth. Someone born on January 10, 1970 turns 65 on January 10, 2035.

What happens if I was born on February 29?

In a milestone year without February 29, the observed birthday may be February 28 or March 1 depending on local law or convention. The calculator shows that the exact observed date can vary instead of presenting one legal date.

Does turning 65 mean I can retire?

Not necessarily. Retirement and pension eligibility depend on country, programme, contributions and personal circumstances. Use the Retirement Calculator for planning estimates and confirm official rules with the appropriate authority.
