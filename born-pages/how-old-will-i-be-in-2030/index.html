How Old Will I Be in 2030?

Enter your birth date to calculate your exact age on any date in 2030 and see how your birthday changes the answer.

Last reviewed: July 26, 2026

Quick answer

Subtract your birth year from 2030. That is the age you will turn during 2030. Before your birthday, you will be one year younger. For example, someone born in 1990 will be 39 before their birthday and 40 on or after their birthday in 2030.

Why can there be two answers?

A year-only calculation tells you the age you will turn. Your exact age on a particular day depends on whether your birthday has already happened. The calculator compares the full calendar dates, including month lengths and leap years.

Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1990?

You will be 39 before your birthday and 40 on or after your birthday in 2030.

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 2000?

You will be 29 before your birthday and 30 on or after your birthday in 2030.

What if I was born on February 29?

The age calculation still follows calendar years. In 2030, which is not a leap year, February 29 birthdays may be observed on February 28 or March 1 depending on local convention. The calculator shows the elapsed calendar time to the date you select.
