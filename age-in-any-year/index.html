How Old Was I in a Specific Year?

Use a birth year for a quick before-and-after birthday answer, or enter two complete dates for an exact calendar age.

Quick answer

Subtract your birth year from the target year. That is the age you turned during the target year. Before your birthday, you were one year younger. For example, 2001 − 1986 = 15, so someone born in 1986 was 14 before their birthday in 2001 and 15 on or after it.

Frequently asked questions

How old was I in 2001 if I was born in 1986?

You were 14 before your birthday in 2001 and 15 on or after your birthday.

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1990?

You will be 39 before your birthday in 2030 and 40 on or after your birthday.

Why can the answer be two different ages?

A year-only calculation does not include your birthday. The older age is the age you turn during the year; the younger age applies before that birthday.

Can I calculate age on an exact historical date?

Yes. Use the complete date-of-birth and target-date fields to calculate calendar years, months and days.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. Total elapsed days use UTC calendar dates. For the years-months-days interval, a February 29 anniversary is treated as February 28 in a non-leap year.

Can the target date be in the future?

Yes, up to December 31, 2100. The same calculation works for past and future dates.
