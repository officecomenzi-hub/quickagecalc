What Is Pensionable Age?

Pensionable age is the age at which a particular public pension or retirement benefit may become payable. It is not necessarily the age at which you stop working. Eligibility may also depend on contributions, residence, employment history or other scheme-specific conditions.

How This Calculator Works

The calculator adds the selected whole-year reference age to your complete birth date. For example, a person born on September 10, 1970 using a reference age of 67 reaches that selected age on September 10, 2037. This date is a calendar estimate only; it does not prove eligibility for a pension benefit.

Early, Full and Delayed Benefits Are Different

Public pension systems use different concepts. In the United States, Social Security retirement benefits can generally start at 62 with a reduction, full retirement age depends on birth year, and delaying beyond full retirement age can increase the benefit up to age 70. Canada similarly allows CPP to start before or after the standard age of 65. Other countries use different rules, so do not treat a single age as universal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the pensionable age in the US?
Social Security full retirement age is 67 for people born in 1960 or later. Earlier birth years have lower full retirement ages under the SSA schedule. Retirement benefits can generally start at 62 with a permanent reduction, while delaying can increase the monthly benefit up to age 70.
How do I calculate my reference retirement year?
Add the selected whole-year reference age to your birth year and keep the same month and day. For example, someone born September 10, 1970 reaches age 67 on September 10, 2037. Official pension eligibility may use more detailed rules.
When can I retire?
You may stop working before, at or after the age at which a public pension becomes available. Employment retirement age, private-pension access and state-pension eligibility are different concepts. Check the rules that apply to your own pension scheme.
What is the pensionable age in the UK?
The UK State Pension age is transitioning from 66 to 67 between April 2026 and April 2028. The exact State Pension age for people in the transition depends on their date of birth, so use the official GOV.UK timetable or checker.
Can I receive a pension before the full or standard age?
That depends on the pension system. U.S. Social Security retirement benefits can generally start at 62 with a reduction, while Canada's CPP can generally start at 60 with a reduction. Other countries and schemes have different early-retirement rules.
How many years until I reach the selected age?
Enter your birth date and select a reference age. The calculator shows complete years and complete months remaining until the calendar date on which you reach that selected age.
Is pensionable age the same as retirement age?
Not necessarily. Pensionable age usually refers to eligibility for a particular pension benefit, while retirement can mean when you stop or reduce work. Singapore, for example, explicitly separates CPF payout eligibility age from its employment retirement age.
